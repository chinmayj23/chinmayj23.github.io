Chinmay Joshi

M.Sc in Computer Science at Saarland University, Germany

I have a broad interest in machine learning and data science, and I have lately been curious about data-centric and reliable deep learning.

My experience spans a range of applied ML problems across research and industry contexts. I have worked extensively with computer vision, including projects on single-image 3D reconstruction, synthetic data generation, pose estimation, and object detection, alongside large-scale experimentation and data analysis, with early exposure to time-series modeling. Across these projects, I’ve had the chance to see data-driven ideas translate into real outcomes - enabling large-scale research experiments, improving everyday workflows for domain experts, or contributing to data-informed decisions.

When I'm not distracted by these endless academic pursuits, I'm busy either pondering philosophy or trying to predict the next Wimbledon champion. Sometimes, you might find me in the mountains with my camera or deep in the forest, lost in the world of birds.
